You can name four favourite films in a second. Now name four essays.

You have read a few dozen pieces that changed how you argue, and no page anywhere carries your name next to them. Log one and it goes on a shelf under your name, where anyone can read it.

Step 01 · The only required tap

Log it

One tap files it under your name and adds it to the essay's log. Add half-stars if you have a view, or leave it blank.

Step 02 · Whenever

Sort your shelves

Everything lands on your default shelf. Move pieces onto named shelves whenever you like. Your shelves are a portrait of how you think, and strangers read them for that.

Step 03 · Optional

Say something

One line, if you have one. Most readers never write a note, and their shelves carry the same weight.

What that looks like on a Tuesday

You finish The Housing Theory of Everything on the 7:40 into Waterloo and tap Log it before you pocket your phone. Four stars, if you feel like it. Three seconds, no review to write.

Nine people follow your shelf. Two read it that week because your name is next to it. In 2031 it still sits near the top of the housing shelf, carrying your stamp among four hundred others.
